# Junkers G 38 visual review

- Three-view plan: [NACA Aircraft Circular No. 116 (1930)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Junkers_G_38_3-view_NACA_Aircraft_Circular_No.116.jpg), public domain. `source-plan.jpg` is the source; `outline.json` records manually traced top-view coordinates. The plan gives 45 m span and 23 m length.
- Blender generated the boss base and damaged frames on the GTX PC. The G 38 was a transport; weapons, durability and boss attacks in this game are fictional. The 160 × 80 game sprite is scaled for play rather than physically scaled against the fighters.
