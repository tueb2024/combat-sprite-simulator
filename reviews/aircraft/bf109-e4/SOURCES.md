# Bf 109 E-4 visual review

- Three-view plan: [Björn Huber, Messerschmitt Bf 109 E 3-seiten](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Messerschmitt_Bf_109_E_3-seiten.jpg), CC BY-SA 2.0 DE. `source-plan.jpg` is the attributed source; `outline.json` records manually traced top-view coordinates. The game render and review JPGs are adapted from this plan and carry the same CC BY-SA terms. The drawing is E-series; E-4 details are simplified.
- Dimensions and identity: *Fighting Aircraft of the World*, Sharpe and Chant, private Aiphonse copy, PDF page 405 / printed page 401; see `assets/aircraft/specs/bf109-e4.json`. No book page is distributed.
- The Blender file and nine bank/propeller frames were generated on the GTX PC; `tools/blender/run_review_fleet_on_gtx.sh` reproduces them. The speed and attack behavior are arcade design, not historical performance data.
