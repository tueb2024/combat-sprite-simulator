- Engine: JumPHP, canonical source `https://git.jumphp.net/jumphp/jumPhp.git`, commit `f9365d7375c804d1ce4bbbd48adabbf7e7e1fe52`.

The game scales and mirrors this small sample to scroll it.

- WWII fighter and dive-bomber sprites: original parametrically generated Blender 5.2.3 meshes and GPU renders on the GTX 1660 SUPER. The [three aircraft factsheets](assets/aircraft/specs/) cite Francis Crosby, *The Complete Guide to Fighters & Bombers of the World* (2008), PDF page 93 / printed page 91 (Hurricane Mk I); Mike Sharpe and Christopher Chant, *Fighting Aircraft of the World* (2004), PDF page 405 / printed page 401 (Bf 109 E family) and PDF page 341 / printed page 337 (Ju 87 B family).
